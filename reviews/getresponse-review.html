GetResponse Review 2026: Full Breakdown for Coaches & Creators

Disclosure: This post contains affiliate links. If you sign up through a link here, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. We only recommend tools we've actually researched and believe are a good fit for the audience we're writing for.

GetResponse has grown from a straightforward email tool into a fairly full marketing platform — website builder, automation, webinars, and course creation all sit alongside its email marketing core now. Here's whether that growth actually helps you or just adds complexity you don't need.

What GetResponse Is

At its core, GetResponse is an email marketing platform with automation workflows, landing pages, and signup forms. Layered on top: a website builder, webinar hosting, an online course creator, basic CRM/tagging tools, and ecommerce features like abandoned cart recovery.

Pricing (2026)

  • Free — $0/month. Up to 500 contacts, premium features for the first 14 days.
  • Starter — from $19/month (1,000 contacts). Unlimited sends, autoresponders, landing pages, a website builder — but only one automation workflow.
  • Marketer — from $59/month (1,000 contacts). Unlimited automation workflows, webinar hosting up to 100 attendees, advanced segmentation.
  • Creator — from $69/month. Adds course-creation tools on top of Marketer.
  • Enterprise (MAX) — custom pricing for large organizations.

Annual billing saves roughly 18%. Annual plans are non-refundable, so it's worth running a few months on monthly billing first to confirm the platform fits.

What It Does Well

Genuinely unlimited email sends, even on the cheapest paid tier — matters if you email frequently.

Webinars are built in from the Marketer tier up, so no separate webinar tool needed.

Reasonably priced against feature-comparable competitors like ActiveCampaign at scale.

Where It Falls Short

Starter's single automation workflow is a real constraint — most creators outgrow it fast and need to jump to Marketer, a $40/month increase.

Pricing scales steeply with list size — on Marketer, cost can climb roughly 9–10x going from 1,000 to 100,000 contacts.

No dedicated landing page builder add-on, and it lacks IP warm-up tools some competitors offer.

Who Should Actually Use This

  • Creators who email frequently and don't want send caps
  • Anyone planning to run webinars as part of their sales process
  • Course creators who want webinar + course tools in one platform

Who Should Skip It

  • Anyone expecting to need more than one automation workflow on a tight budget
  • Businesses planning rapid list growth, where pricing jumps could get expensive fast
  • Marketers who specifically need a standalone landing page builder with more design flexibility

Bottom Line

GetResponse earns its price at the Marketer tier and up — unlimited automation, built-in webinars, and solid deliverability for creators who email often. The free and Starter tiers are worth trying, but budget realistically: most people outgrow Starter's single automation limit fast.
